Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

Reverse Price ("the App") does not collect any personal information from users. The App only processes numerical data required for calculations such as profit, fees, and shipping costs. This data is used solely within the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers.

2. Data Storage

The App may store calculation results and settings locally on the user's device. This data is intended to improve the convenience of the App and will not be shared with third parties.
